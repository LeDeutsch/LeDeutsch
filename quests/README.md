## Available adventures

### [Diagnose the Iron Beast](nethardware/) —  Rank S
A mechanical creature is dying. **Diagnose** its ailment before it goes silent.
> *Real project: [nethardware-monitor](https://github.com/LeDeutsch/nethardware-monitor) — real-time PC hardware supervision.*

### 🈁 [Translate the Runes of Aincrad](sao-utils/) —  Rank A
A grimoire from another world waits to be deciphered. **Prove your mastery of the ancient tongue.**
> *Real project: [SAO-Utils-Patch-lang-fr](https://github.com/LeDeutsch/SAO-Utils-Patch-lang-fr) — FR translation of SAO Utils.*

### [Enchant the Seals of Passage](estiam-rfid/) —  Rank A
An arcane door refuses all intruders. **Find the right seal to open it.**
> *Real project: [Estiam-RFID](https://github.com/LeDeutsch/Estiam-RFID) — RFID badge authentication.*

### [Order the Unity Grimoire](unity-hierarchy/) —  Rank B
A chaotic tome needs to be sorted. **Bring order among the runes.**
> *Real project: [custom-hierarchy-Unity](https://github.com/LeDeutsch/custom-hierarchy-Unity) — Unity plugin.*

### [Weave the Veils of Style](lulu/) —  Rank B
A shapeless illusion seeks its garment. **Help it become visible.**
> *Real project: [LULU](https://github.com/LeDeutsch/LULU) — CSS experimentation.*

## Adventurer's Card

Every completed quest = one stamp on your card.
Come back to the guild after each victory, the hostess keeps count.

- **0/5** quests done → *Curious Traveler*
- **3/5** → *Guild Companion*
- **5/5** → *Honorary Member*  → **hidden quest unlocked**

*(Honor system, the stamps live in your heart, not in a database.)*
